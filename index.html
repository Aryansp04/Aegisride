🗣️ NLP Language
NOT USED Intentionally Excluded.
🛡️ Key Defense:

1. Physical impossibility: In severe crashes, the rider is concussed, unconscious, or asphyxiated—rendering speech impossible.
2. Acoustic pollution: Road ambient noise (>85dB at 60 km/h) and wind shear cause high audio error rates.
3. Zero value-add: Kinematics is pure vector math ($m/s^2, rad/s$). Adding NLP adds latency, cost, and battery drain without saving lives.

USED Autonomous Triage & Dispatch Orchestrator:

A deterministic state-machine agent that triggers the progressive 30-second haptic/alarm override sequence. If no human override occurs, it dynamically queries the National Emergency API (108), calculates estimated trauma severity based on delta-V and roll angle, and routes live GPS telemetry to the nearest trauma center with automatic SMS/webhook fallbacks.

Slide 12: Stress-Test Your Design All 4 Pillars & 9 Questions

Stress-Test Your Design: The 4 Critical Pillars

Professors probe systems across Data, Intelligence, Limitations, and Ethics. Here is our bulletproof breakdown.

🗄️

Data

Pillar 01
Question 1

What data does it need?

Kinematic Vector Telemetry:

  • Tri-axial linear acceleration ($A_x, A_y, A_z$ in $m/s^2$ / $G$-force) sampled at 100 Hz.
  • Tri-axial angular velocity (roll, pitch, yaw rate in $^\circ/s$).
  • GNSS/GPS coordinates (lat/long, ground speed in km/h, heading, timestamp).
  • Static local metadata: Rider blood group, vehicle registration, emergency contact phone numbers.
Question 2

Where does it come from?

Edge Hardware Pod: 6-DOF Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU, InvenSense MPU-6050 / ST LSM6DSOX) mounted rigidly to the motorcycle frame or embedded in a smart helmet. GNSS/GPS from an onboard ceramic patch antenna module (NEO-M8N) with auxiliary Bluetooth LE bridge to the rider's smartphone.

🧠

Intelligence

Pillar 02
Question 3

What does it decide or predict?

  • Crash vs False Alarm ($P > 0.92$): Classifies 400ms temporal waveforms to differentiate genuine high-G collisions from non-fatal road noise (potholes, speed bumps, dropped phone/helmet).
  • Trauma Severity Score (TSS): Predicts injury severity (Low / Moderate / Code-Red Trauma) by indexing $\Delta V$ impact deceleration against resting roll angle.
Question 4

What happens next?

Autonomous Triage & Escalation:
1. Fires progressive 30-second escalating haptic & 95dB acoustic alarm on the handlebar/phone.
2. If uncancelled, synthesizes an encrypted JSON emergency packet and dispatches direct CAD API call to the nearest 108 trauma network and family WhatsApp/SMS.

⚠️

Limitations

Pillar 03
Question 5

What could go wrong?

1. Zero Cellular Connectivity: In rural dark zones, 4G drops. Mitigation: 2G GSM SMS / USSD fallback (operates on 1-bar signal) + local 110dB siren & hazard strobes.

2. Battery Severed in Impact: Mitigation: Supercapacitor buffer maintains 60s backup power to complete SOS telemetry transmission.

Question 6

What if the data is wrong?

Sensor Noise & Glitches: Accelerometer saturation or GPS multipath drift in tunnels. Mitigation: Extended Kalman Filtering (EKF) and cross-sensor plausibility checks. A crash requires simultaneous multi-axis covariance: linear deceleration AND angular inversion.

Question 7

What if the prediction is wrong?

False Positive (Says crash, but safe): Cancelled by rider during the mandatory 30-second override window.

False Negative (Misses real crash): Fail-safe rule: If deceleration $>6G$ AND resting roll $>75^\circ$, the system triggers the 30s override sequence unconditionally.

⚖️

Ethics

Pillar 04
Question 8

Could it create a privacy problem?

Risk: Constant GPS tracking feels like surveillance (especially for gig-economy delivery riders).

Our Safeguard (Zero-Cloud Continuous Tracking): GPS and motion vectors are processed strictly in local volatile RAM in a 5-second circular ring buffer. Zero telemetry is logged, stored, or sent to cloud servers during normal rides. Data transmits ONLY upon an uncancelled, verified crash.

Question 9

Could it be biased or unfair?

Risk: ML trained only on smooth Western highways or expensive sport bikes would trigger false alarms on rough Indian roads or economic 100cc mopeds.

Our Safeguard: Dataset trained across diverse Indian road topographies (potholes, monsoon ruts, unpaved dirt) across both commuter mopeds and heavy delivery bikes, ensuring equal reliability regardless of rider economic status.
