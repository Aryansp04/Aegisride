Stress-Test Your Design: The 4 Critical Pillars

Addressing all 9 cross-examination questions across Data, Intelligence, Limitations, and Ethics.

Data
Question 1
What data does it need?
3-axis linear acceleration (G-force), 3-axis angular rates (deg/s), GNSS coordinates (lat/long, speed), and local blood group profile.
Question 2
Where does it come from?
Hardware 6-DOF IMU mounted to bike frame or helmet, plus GNSS module with smartphone BLE bridge.
Intelligence
Question 3
What does it decide/predict?
Crash vs Anomaly ($P>0.92$) via temporal waveform analysis; computes Trauma Severity Score (TSS) from impact energy ΔV.
Question 4
What happens next?
Triggers 30s escalating haptic/audio countdown on bike; if uncancelled, autonomously dispatches SOS packet to 108 Emergency.
Limitations
Question 5
What could go wrong?
No cell signal: 2G SMS fallback (1-bar signal) + 110dB siren & strobes. Battery severance: 60s supercapacitor backup.
Question 6 & 7
Data or prediction wrong?
Sensor noise filtered via Kalman EKF. False alarms cancelled by rider in 30s; severe impacts have hard-coded G>6 fail-safe.
Ethics
Question 8
Could it create privacy problems?
Zero-Cloud Continuous Tracking: Location lives in 5-second circular RAM buffer. Zero route logs or history stored unless crash confirmed.
Question 9
Biased or unfair?
Model trained across Indian road topographies (potholes, monsoon ruts, unpaved) across 100cc mopeds through cargo bikes.
